Zivo POS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to Zivo POS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Zivo mobile application.

Information We Collect

Zivo is designed to operate primarily on your device. The application may store the following information locally:

  • Shop information (name, address, phone, email, GST number)
  • Inventory and product information
  • Invoice and billing records
  • Application settings and preferences
  • Barcode information entered or scanned by the user

How We Use Information

The information stored in Zivo is used exclusively to:

  • Manage inventory and products
  • Create and maintain invoices
  • Store shop information
  • Provide barcode scanning functionality
  • Maintain application settings
  • Improve the overall user experience

Local Data Storage

Zivo stores business data locally on your device using secure local storage technologies. We do not upload your inventory, invoice, or shop information to our servers.

Camera Permission

Zivo may request access to your device camera solely for barcode scanning purposes.

Images and videos are not stored, collected, or transmitted by Zivo.

Biometric Authentication

If enabled, Zivo may use device authentication methods such as:

  • Fingerprint Authentication
  • Face Authentication
  • Device PIN, Password, or Pattern

Biometric data is handled by your device operating system. Zivo does not collect, access, or store biometric information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or business information with third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information stored within the application. However, users remain responsible for securing their devices and maintaining backups of important business data.

Children's Privacy

Zivo is intended for business and productivity use and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.
